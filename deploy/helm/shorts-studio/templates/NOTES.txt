Shorts Studio is available inside the cluster at:

  http://{{ include "shorts-studio.fullname" . }}:{{ .Values.service.port }}

The chart targets a single CPU Deployment with durable /data storage. A
non-empty SHORTS_API_TOKEN is REQUIRED (the app refuses unauthenticated
non-loopback binds); set it through --set-string
'secretEnv.SHORTS_API_TOKEN=...' (or an externally managed Secret keyed into
secretEnv). The published CPU image supports amd64 and arm64; CUDA/GPU is not
part of this chart.
